**Fix: forecast wording after midnight.**

- When the dropdown predicts you will run out in the small hours (before 5am), it now says **"tonight"** instead of **"tomorrow night"**. For example, if your weekly limit resets at 4:00 am and you're on course to run out at 2 am, it no longer sounds like you'd run out on the evening *after* the reset.
- The forecast maths is unchanged; only the wording of the time of day.

**Install:** download `ClaudeUsage-1.4.1.zip`, unzip, and move `ClaudeUsage.app` to `/Applications`. The app is only ad-hoc signed, so the first time, right-click it and choose **Open**. macOS may also ask for your Mac password so the app can read its saved sign-in from your Keychain: click **Always Allow**. Upgrading: open **About** and it will offer this update; otherwise quit the old app first, then replace it.
